# 1.2.7 CX Enterprise Coworker and Workfront

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
>Before you begin, read the below instructions!

## Instructions for in-person workshops

For this exercise, you need to use:

- **Instance**: **Adobe Tech Insiders**
- **Sandbox**: **One Adobe**
- **Dataview**: **AdobeOne - Unified Customer Data View**
- **Username**: **adobetechinsiders-XXX@adobeeventlab.com** and replace XXX by the number that was assigned to you
- **Password**: use the password that was shared with you

You've now completed this lab.

## 1.2.7.1 Accessing CX Enterprise Coworker

Go to [https://experience.adobe.com](https://experience.adobe.com). Click to open Coworker.

![Coworker - Workfront](./images/cw_wf1.png)

After logging in, click the 3 dots **...** in the bottom left part of the screen next to your username and make sure you're in the org `--aepImsOrgName--` and in the sandbox `--aepSandboxName--` by selecting them as indicated below.

![Coworker - Workfront](./images/cw_wf2.png)

## 1.2.7.3 Approvals

![Coworker - Workfront](./images/cw_wf30.png)

Download this file: [timetravelnow.png](./images/timetravelnow.png) to your desktop.

![Coworker - Workfront](./images/cw_wf31.png)
