# Schedule streaming capacity

Learn how to schedule additional streaming capacity when you need it most.

Major business events such as sales and product launches bring more traffic to your brand and you may need additional streaming capacity in Adobe Experience Platform to keep up with this surge in traffic. By anticipating needs and allocating more capacity during these busy periods, you can avoid performance degradation. Having enough capacity is necessary to make sure streaming services continue to be real-time.
