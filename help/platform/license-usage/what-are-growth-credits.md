# What are growth credits?

Growth credits allow customers of Adobe Experience Platform-based applications like Real-Time CDP and Journey Optimizer to connect eligible entitlements to how they allocate capacity, what they consume, and how they scale as business needs change.

>[!AVAILABILITY]
>
> Growth credits are applicable only to customers of Real-Time CDP and/or Adobe Journey Optimizer who license Experience Platform growth credits.
