# Monitor and address growth credit overage

Learn how to interpret and respond to growth credit overages. Specifically, you'll see how to use the credit usage dashboard to understand consumption and determine whether growth was expected, how to recognize when you're blocked from scheduling additional capacity due to overage, and how to subscribe to proactive credit overage alerts. 

You'll also learn what actually happens once you're in overage — including how enforcement differs between streaming/edge capacity and batch audience evaluation — and walk through the five ways to resolve an overage: releasing reserved credits, shortening a schedule's date range, reducing capacity, redistributing baseline capacity across sandboxes, and optimizing consumption, plus when purchasing additional credits makes sense as a last resort. 

>[!AVAILABILITY]
>
> Growth credits are applicable only to customers of Real-Time CDP and/or Adobe Journey Optimizer who license Experience Platform growth credits.
